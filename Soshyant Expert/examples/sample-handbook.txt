Sample operations handbook — demonstration documents only

Equipment identification
The fictional Aurora X200 is a circulation pump. Use the model and serial number when reporting an incident.

Warranty
The Aurora X200 warranty is 36 months from the installation date. Claims require the installation report and the pump serial number.

Error codes
Error E-04 indicates a dry-run condition. The documented reset procedure is to restore inlet pressure and hold the reset button for 3 seconds.

Escalation
Unresolved incidents after two site visits must be escalated to the regional engineering manager. Record the serial number, firmware version and last three error codes.

Demonstration notice
These fictional instructions are test fixtures. They must not be used to operate real equipment.
