# Field Support Playbook — Aurora product line

## Escalation policy
Any pump fault that remains unresolved after two site visits escalates to the regional engineering manager. Report the serial number, firmware version, and the last three error codes in the ticket. Critical incidents (flooding, fire risk, production line stoppage) skip escalation and page the on-call engineer directly.

## Customer communication rules
Never quote a repair cost on site; costs are issued by the service desk within 24 hours using the standard rate card. Always leave the printed checklist with the customer after a visit. If a customer asks about warranty coverage, confirm the installation date from the installation report before promising anything.

## Safety notices
Before any service on the X200 or X400 pumps, isolate electrical supply and verify zero energy with the two-step lockout procedure described in the corporate safety standard. Pumps that handled glycol mixtures above 30 percent require chemical-resistant gloves and face shield during seal replacement.
