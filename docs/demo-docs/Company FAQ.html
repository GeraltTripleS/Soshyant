Aurora FAQ

How often should the X200 be serviced?

Standard service interval is every 6,000 running hours or 12 months, whichever comes first. Heavy-duty applications (water above 60 degrees Celsius) halve the interval.

Can the X200 run with a closed inlet valve?

No. Running against a closed inlet causes error E-04 dry-run shutdown within 10 seconds and can destroy the mechanical seal.

What firmware is current?

Controller firmware 2.8.1 is the current release as of this revision. Units on 2.6.x or older must be updated by a certified technician.
