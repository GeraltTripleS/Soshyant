Aurora X200 Circulation Pump — Service Manual (Revision 4)

Section 3: Impeller Bolt Installation
The impeller housing is secured with six M12 grade 8.8 bolts. Tighten in a star pattern in two passes: first pass to 30 Nm, second pass to 60 Nm. Re-check torque after the first heat cycle (approximately 50 hours of operation). Never apply threadlocker to these bolts; the housing gasket requires dry torque.

Section 5: Warranty
The Aurora X200 carries a 36-month limited warranty from the date of installation, covering manufacturing defects in the housing, impeller, and motor windings. Damage caused by dry running, cavitation, or unauthorized disassembly voids the warranty. Warranty claims require the installation report and the pump serial number.

Section 8: Error Codes
Error E-04 indicates a dry-run condition: the pump has operated without sufficient inlet pressure for more than 10 seconds. The controller shuts the motor down to protect the seal. To clear E-04, restore inlet pressure and hold the reset button for 3 seconds.
Error E-11 signals bearing temperature above 95 degrees Celsius. Allow the pump to cool, inspect the lubrication level per Section 6, and restart. If E-11 recurs within 24 hours, replace the bearing cartridge.

Section 11: Recommended Spares
Every installation should hold one bearing cartridge (part A-114), one housing gasket set (part G-207), and two mechanical seals (part S-330). Facilities running more than ten X200 units should also stock one complete motor assembly (part M-055).
